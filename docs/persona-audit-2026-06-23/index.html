01Executive summary

Implementation update: all 100 persona rows now pass locally. The original audit found a strong acquisition surface but severe value-delivery failures after the buying, download, resume, team-inquiry, demo, and certificate moments. Those failure paths are now locally remediated in code and covered with focused regression tests.

Original audit baseline: 58 of 100 personas failed, bounced, or were stranded, including 23 critical failures. The issues clustered after the click that matters, in value-delivery rather than raw click counts. The six money-and-trust loss points were:

  1. The $295 certificate was unreachable in the audit — locally remediated; live Vercel PDF/email proof remains.
  2. Paid buyers got stranded — locally remediated with passwordless recovery, resend links, stranded-buyer detection, and support intake; live email proof remains.
  3. No retention loop existed — locally remediated with assessment drafts, resume reminders, paid not-started reminders, stalled-module reminders, and In-Depth waiting reminders; live cron/email proof remains.
  4. Team buyers ready to pay got a mailto: — locally remediated with structured inquiry flows, launch packets, SLA copy, inbox notification, and support-case creation; live delivery proof remains.
  5. The interactive demos were convincing fakes — locally remediated with governed public model endpoints and honest practice/playground labeling; live model-key proof remains.
  6. Email-gated free downloads broke after capture — locally remediated with committed-PDF success gates, static PDF streams, and failure states; live PDF proof remains.
Current status: persona-row remediation is locally complete. Remaining proof is production-level: Vercel env/redeploy, Stripe/manual-refund workflow, cron/email delivery, PDF generation, public model-key behavior, and seeded/live data validation.

02Largest gaps P0 · fix first

Business-breaking failures — places where a user does everything right and gets nothing, or where the system silently loses money. Ranked.

GAP 1 The $295 certificate was unreachable — local remediation shipped; production proof remains.

Implementation update 2026-06-23: work-product submission now auto-approves after all 18 modules are complete, issues a certificate idempotently, sends the certificate email on first issuance, the dashboard only shows “Verified” when a real certificate row exists, and /verify has a certificate-ID lookup page. Remaining work is live Vercel proof for the certificate/PDF/email paths.

Original finding: every learner who finished all 18 modules and submitted their work product (personas 10, 37, 48, 60, 78, 94, 100) waited forever.
  • Nothing set work_submissions.review_status='approved' — no reviewer UI, cron, or auto-approve.
  • Nothing called the certificate issuance path.
  • Even if approved, the cert PDF used @react-pdf renderToBuffer → React #31 500.
  • /submit promised an issuance email that no route sent.
  • The dashboard falsely showed “Verified ✓” the moment modules completed.
  • There was no /verify lookup entry point for third parties.
Action · ✅ local auto-approval + certificate issuance M · prove cert/report/AUP PDFs on Vercel preview M · ✅ first-issuance certificate email S · ✅ dashboard credential truth + /verify lookup S

GAP 2 Paid buyers get stranded — access depends on one email bank gateways filter.

$99 & $295 fulfillment is a password-less account + a single magic-link email (personas 2, 25, 44, 65, 86, 98). For Mimecast/Proofpoint banks — the exact audience:
  • If the email is filtered, the buyer has a provisioned account they can’t reach.
  • /auth/login requires a password the account never set; device-confirm is same-browser-only (a 2nd-device returner loops).
  • The webhook only emails on action==='created' — a failed first send + Stripe retry is a dedup no-op, never re-sends.
  • No “stranded buyer” detection — ops can’t tell stranded from lazy.
Action · make login lead with passwordless/magic-link M · make device-confirm cross-device-tolerant M · add a self-serve “I bought something but can’t get in” recovery M · flag enrolled && never authenticated + alert ops S

GAP 3 Retention loop was absent — local reminders shipped; production proof remains.

Implementation update 2026-06-23: assessment drafts/resume reminders and paid-product re-engagement are implemented locally. Foundation stalled reminders are module-specific, so Module 3 abandoners get pulled back to their current module. Remaining work is live migration, cron, and email proof.

Original finding: there was no re-engagement anywhere (only cleanup-rate-limits + pdf-cleanup crons).
  • Free-assessment abandoners were sessionStorage-only, email was captured only after Q12, “Start over” re-rolled a fresh question set, and cross-device return was structurally impossible.
  • Idle $99 buyers and never-start $295 buyers were never contacted again.
  • Module-3 abandoners had persisted progress but no pull-back reminder.
Action · ✅ server-side assessment drafts/resume link M · ✅ transactional paid re-engagement reminders M · ✅ stable question set on resume/restart S · prove cron/email delivery in production M

GAP 4 Team buyers ready to pay get a mailto:, not a checkout.

Nine personas want team training; the four most ready-to-buy (55, 73, 81, 91) hit a wall.
  • /assessment/team copy literally says checkout is “intentionally gated until two production-like cohorts pass QA.”
  • Every team CTA on /for-institutions is a raw mailto: — no form, CRM, scheduler, or SLA.
  • The full self-serve Stripe machinery exists but is flag-dark.
  • The team-report preview is linked only via a mobile-only deep link.
Action · replace every mailto: with a lead form + Calendly + “reply within 1 business day” S · remove the QA-gating rationale from buyer copy S · finish hardening & flip the self-serve flag (or present a clean assisted motion) M · surface the team preview on desktop S

GAP 5 The interactive demos are convincing fakes that destroy credibility with the best prospects.

/playground and /practice are canned mockups (personas 9, 18, 45, 64, 96).
  • “Run” is a typewriter over hardcoded output; “Save to Toolbox” persists nothing; the “recent” panel is fiction.
  • /practice falsely brands itself “Signed-in sandbox / Enrolled-only” while public, and offers a .md download of fabricated AI output.
  • The real working loop (/dashboard/toolbox, “AiBI Lab”) is buried behind auth+payment and not in nav.
  • Both are orphaned; ~2,463 lines of orphaned dead code sit alongside.
Action · wire to the real (rate-limited, PII-scanned) model OR clearly label “interactive preview” + link the real product M · remove the false “Enrolled-only” claim & fake export S · give the real Toolbox a discoverable entry S · delete the orphaned dead code S

GAP 6 Every email-gated free download is broken — surrender an email, get a 500.

The only two email-gated free assets are the only two using @react-pdf renderToBuffer (personas 7, 16, 28, 33, 47, 56, 63).
  • Originally, Prompt Cards PDF and Safe AI Use Guide both 500ed in prod (React #31).
  • Locally fixed: both stream committed PDFs; success/unlock waits for a successful PDF response; Prompt Cards role selection matches the server allowlist.
  • Related static course/resource PDF render risk has been reduced locally by serving committed files for the highest-risk downloads.
Action · move all renderToBuffer downloads to the working Chromium/print path (or pre-generate static PDFs) M · fix the Prompt Cards role dropdown S · don’t show “unlocked”/“success” until the file streams S
