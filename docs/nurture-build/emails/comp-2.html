A policy on a shared drive is documentation. Defensible is different: for any AI-assisted output, you can show the owner, the data line (what could and could not touch the tool), and the review path that put a human between the model and the consequence — with enough of a record to reconstruct it later.

Pick one AI use and write those three lines. The gap you can’t fill is the one worth fixing first. This is the same discipline behind current risk, model-risk, and third-party oversight expectations — applied where the AI use actually creates consequence.
