Now that you have the kit, here is the part most teams skip. Policies get written and filed. What actually creates control is simpler — three lines on every AI use:

Owner: who is accountable? Data line: what data is allowed or prohibited? Review path: who checks the output before it reaches a customer, a file, or a retained record?

Try it on one AI use you already know exists. If you cannot fill one in, that is not a failure — that is the gap the policy was supposed to surface. That is the difference between “we have an AI policy” and “we can show how AI work is controlled.”
