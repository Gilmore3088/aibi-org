For a community bank or credit union, the AI capability ladder does not need to be complicated. Three rungs handle most institutions through $5 billion in assets:

Foundation

Every banker who touches workflow improvement. Knows the sanctioned tool, the data rules, the use-case inventory process, and the prompt patterns that actually work in regulated work. Target: one in five of your team within 12 months.

Specialist

Domain leads in operations, lending, deposits, or compliance who own end-to-end use-case design and the impact ledger for their function. Target: one per major function.

Leader

The named accountable executive (often the COO or CIO) who owns governance, vendor selection, and the board-level conversation. Target: one per institution.
