If you’re presenting an AI plan to your board this quarter, three sourced numbers are worth memorizing:

•  66% of banks are discussing AI budget allocation (Bank Director 2024 Technology Survey, via Jack Henry)

•  ~65% is the median efficiency ratio for community banks (FDIC CEIC data, 1992–2025)

•  ~56.0% is the industry-wide efficiency ratio (FDIC Quarterly Banking Profile, first quarter of 2026)

That efficiency-ratio spread is not a promise that AI training closes the gap by itself. It is a useful board conversation starter: where are manual back-office workflows creating avoidable drag, and which ones can safely become machine-assisted with a human in the loop on every output?
