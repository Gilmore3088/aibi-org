Quick follow-up.

The single largest institutional risk in a Ready to Scale AI program is concentration. If three people on your team hold all the AI capability, the program is one resignation away from a setback. The fix is not hiring. The fix is credentialing the bench.

The operating model I would design toward:

Foundation holders in each branch or major function

For many institutions, that starts with 10–25 Foundation-trained bankers. They become the named first responders for any new use case in their area.

Specialist capability by business line

Operations, lending, deposits, compliance. As Specialist tracks come online or are scoped by request, each function should own end-to-end use-case design and the impact ledger for its work.

A named accountable executive for AI

The executive owner needs clear authority to speak to the board and the regulator with equal fluency. This is the role that interfaces with examiners and signs the one-page memo.

Institutional Foundation rollouts are scoped by request: seat count, success metrics, and the sequence for credentialing the bench before broader AI expansion.
