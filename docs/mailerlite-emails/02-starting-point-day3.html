Quick follow-up.

If you took the use-case registry suggestion from earlier this week, you already know more than most community-bank executives about what AI is actually doing inside their walls. The next move is one banker, one hour, one piece of real work.

Three pilots you can run in an approved AI workspace or a non-production sandbox — never with confidential vendor, customer, employee, or examination-sensitive content:

Summarize a credit memo

Paste a redacted, non-PII memo. Ask for a 200-word executive summary plus three risk flags with section citations.

Redline a procedure

Paste an old procedure. Ask for changes that align it to current FFIEC guidance language. Note every change before sending it for compliance review.

Compare two vendor proposals

Paste both (non-confidential versions). Ask for a side-by-side decision matrix on cost, integration, and exit clauses. The output beats a printed spreadsheet most of the time.

The point is not the output. The point is what the banker says afterward: “where else could this save us 20 minutes?” That sentence is how adoption starts in community banking.
