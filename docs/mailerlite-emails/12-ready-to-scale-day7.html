Ready to Scale · Day 7

One honest caveat about your Ready to Scale result.

It is one person’s read. You answered twelve questions about your institution, and you answered them well. But at this maturity, the biggest risk is rarely what leadership sees. It is the gap between the executive view and how AI is actually being used at the teller line, in the loan file, and in the back office.

That gap is hard to see from the corner office, and it is the kind of gap an exam can surface. Three questions only your team can answer:

Does practice match policy?

Your acceptable-use policy says one thing. The question is whether the people using AI every day know it, follow it, and know where the boundaries are.

Where is the capability, really?

A mature program can still be concentrated in a few people. A team read shows which departments and roles carry the skill, and which are borrowing it.

What would you show the board?

“I believe we are ready” is an opinion. A team-wide baseline, broken down by department and role, is evidence.
