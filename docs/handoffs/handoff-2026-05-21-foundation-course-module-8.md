## The thesis (never lose this)
We are building **"safe professional confidence,"** not "AI literacy." The audience is overwhelmed, non-technical community bankers who fear getting it wrong. **Reduce fear / grant emotional permission before adding complexity.** The course's defining strength is that it stays **calm** — leverage rises while emotional friction stays flat.

## The staircase so far
1 approachable → 2 judgment → 3 better outputs → 4 use it myself → 5 make it reusable → 6 work through documents → 7 which tool to reach for. **M8 = the next rung up in leverage.**

## The locked module pattern (match it exactly)
Every module = ~12–15 min (M7 ~10), calm, plain-English, low visual density, and roughly these beats:
1. **Feel seen first** — a painfully specific banking workplace moment + a reframe line. Recognition before explanation.
2. **The idea, plain** — one simple mental model, no jargon/acronyms.
3. **See it happen** — a believable, slightly-imperfect banking example (side-by-side / before-after). Output is never robotically perfect.
4. **Micro-check** — learner performs a judgment (builds the skill; "no wrong answer" tone, with a reveal).
5. **Practice once** — sandbox only if it earns its place; otherwise demonstration. Constrained, impossible to fail.
6. **Keep an artifact** (Toolbox) **+ identity close** (a calm, non-hype identity statement).

## Non-negotiable rules
- **No branded frameworks/acronyms** in learner copy (no TCREI, no "4D"). Plain-English recipes that read as common sense.
- **Tone discipline is the #1 risk** in the back half — never drift to whitepaper/keynote/AI-influencer voice, no hype, no "future of AI," no "replace your job," no startup language.
- **Increase leverage faster than complexity.** Escalate agency gradually (M4 press Run → M5 modify a reusable brief → M8 chain a few steps).
- **Sandbox is core but selective**; synthetic/non-sensitive data only; "we pay for it"; multi-provider (Claude/OpenAI/Gemini), owner-funded.
- **Completion-based, ungraded. Tool-agnostic.** Toolbox = the learner's growing "personal AI operating layer."
- Keep visual density LOW. Believable, messy-but-readable examples. Real banking specificity, never fake-LMS.

## Signature framings to reuse (carry forward aggressively)
- "You use it and you check it" / "the tool drafts, you decide" / "you stay in the chair" (human judgment; from M2).
- "Translator, not summarizer" · "focused beats firehose" (M6).
- "You're not marrying a tool" / "switching is normal" (M4, M7).
- Verification = professional skill, not fear; **sources = faster verification, NOT truth** (protect M2).
- "Better prompting improves reviewability, not guaranteed truth" (M3).
- Recurring metaphor: AI as a **"confident new hire"** (capable, useful, still needs review).
- "Practice with made-up or non-sensitive examples only" (recurring safety habit — behavioral, not legalistic. The paste test: "Would I be fine if this showed up outside the bank?").

## Module 8 — Agents & Workflow Thinking — drafting guidance
This is the **meatiest** module and the one most likely to break the calm. Draft with a full context budget.

- **Learner question / job:** *"I can string a few steps together — with myself checking at the right moments — so AI helps with a small repeatable process, not just one task."*
- **The trap to avoid:** drifting into technical "agents," autonomous-AI fear, automation sprawl, or sci-fi. Keep it grounded: an "agent/workflow" here is just **a few steps chained, with human checkpoints** — design it before you automate anything.
- **Likely plain framing (NOT the word "agent" as jargon):** "map the steps: what goes in → what AI does → **where you check** → next step → when to escalate." Human gates are the heart of it. Strong continuity with M2 (judgment) and the "you stay in the chair" line.
- **Scope = the individual only.** Foundation tops out at *your own* small multi-step workflow (e.g., a lender's own draft chain). Cross-department / team / institutional agents are explicitly **future AiBI-S/L**, out of scope (per earlier decisions + launch spec).
- **Existing content to build on** (`content/courses/foundation-program/module-8.ts` + v4): "Map an AI workflow — input, AI task, output, human checkpoint, escalation boundary — before automating." Artifact = **Workflow Map.**
- **Title:** "Agents & Workflow Thinking" may be too technical/abstract for the course voice — consider a plainer learner-facing title (e.g., "Stringing Steps Together," "Your First Workflow," "Letting AI Handle a Few Steps") and flag it for the owner, keeping the internal id. (Same pattern as M5 → "Teaching AI Your Style," M7 → "Which Tool, When?")
- **Sandbox?** Optional/light — could show a 2–3 step workflow run with a visible human-checkpoint stop. If used, keep it impossible-to-fail. Demonstration is also fine.
- **Identity close direction:** something like *"you're not just using AI — you're designing how it fits into your work, with you in control."*
