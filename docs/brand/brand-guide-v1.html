01 · Naming

One name people can read. One mark we can grow into.

The public-facing name is always the full Institute name. AiBI is shorthand for credentials and products; the bracketed mark is a visual device, not a name to decode. Don't make a new visitor solve a puzzle before they understand the offer.

Master brand
Ai Banking InstituteUse in full on first reference, in nav, in legal copy, and anywhere a new reader needs clarity.
Short reference
the InstituteFor second and later references inside the same piece of copy.
Credential / product shorthand
AiBI — AiBI-Foundation, AiBI-S, AiBI-LAcceptable in product UI, credential codes, and internal use. Not a replacement for the master brand in body prose.
Visual mark
[Ai] Banking InstituteThe primary lockup is the open wordmark — no box. Gold brackets wrap the "Ai"; the lowercase "i" is a single Instrument Serif italic glyph, one color, no tittle — the only stylized detail. The compact [Ai] monogram is reserved for app icons, favicons, and tight footers. The mark is decoration, never spoken or set in running text.

Pronunciation: say it as "Ai Banking Institute." The mark is never read aloud letter-by-letter in customer-facing settings.

02 · Voice

Specifics beat adjectives.

We sound like a credible banking institution that happens to teach Ai — not an Ai company that happens to sell to banks. Plain, accountable, and specific. No hype, no exclamation points, no emoji, no "Ai-powered."

Trait 01

Precise

Name the role, the task, the artifact. A concrete noun beats a superlative every time.

Trait 02

Accountable

Say what we do and don't do. Claims are scoped to what the product can stand behind today.

Trait 03

Restrained

No urgency theater, no growth-hacking tone. Confidence is shown by what we leave out.

Trait 04

Banking-specific

Examples come from compliance, lending, BSA/AML, branch, and risk — not generic "knowledge work."

Say this

  • Ai-assisted, Ai-supported, review-ready, examiner-readable
  • Role-based, governance-aware, banking-specific, practical
  • "The free assessment screens 12 readiness signals and returns a score, tier, top gap, and starter artifact."
  • "Designed to be reviewable by compliance, risk, audit, or leadership."

Not this

  • Ai-powered, revolutionary, next-generation, game-changing
  • Regulator-approved, examiner-approved, guaranteed compliant
  • "Deeply scores 12 dimensions" (one question per signal is a screen, not a diagnostic)
  • "Ready for examiner submission" or "will satisfy examiners"

06 · Product UI

Editorial for moments. Plain for the work.

Large type and gold earn attention on covers and credentials. The places where busy bankers do work need clarity before poetry — a working product, not a magazine.

Editorial register

Where weight & gold lead

  • Marketing and hero sections
  • Certificates and completion credentials
  • Report covers and research pieces
  • Brand moments and section dividers
Product register

Where the UI gets out of the way

  • Assessment flow and question screens
  • Toolbox, resource library, and downloads
  • Course lessons and the practice sandbox
  • Checkout, dashboards, and mobile cards

Rule of thumb: in editorial moments, larger Inter weights and gold accents are welcome. When a banker is doing — answering, filtering, downloading, paying — drop to plain Inter, generous hit targets, and clear state. Navigation inside the product is real product navigation, never a brand puzzle.

08 · Claims policy

What we can stand behind.

This is the spine of the brand's credibility with a banking audience. The operating principle below is one of our strongest, safest lines — use it often.

The operating principle

Ai drafts. Humans review. The institution owns the decision.

Claims about Ai & agents

Safe: Ai can draft, summarize, structure, compare, brainstorm, classify, and prepare work for review. The course introduces agent concepts and bounded autonomy — an agent may draft work, flag uncertainty, and pause for human review.

Hold the line: Ai should not make final credit, compliance, legal, BSA/AML, fraud, authentication, or regulatory decisions without qualified human review. The course does not replace compliance, legal, InfoSec, or model-risk review.

Governance alignment

The curriculum is designed around banking obligations such as model risk, third-party risk, fair lending, privacy, and human review — aligned with themes from SR 11-7, Interagency Third-Party Risk guidance, ECOA / Regulation B, and BSA/AML data sensitivity.

Aligned with and designed around are the verbs. Never "approved," "certified," "recognized," or "meets all requirements."

Maturity tense matters. If a feature works end-to-end today, write "the site includes…". If it is still being built, write "the platform is designed to include…". Don't claim full LMS maturity, working institutional dashboards, or aggregate rollups until they actually function.

What AiBI becomes when operational

North-star. Requires real infrastructure before any claim.

  • Reviewer-of-record credentialing
  • Published reviewer roster
  • Certificate verification and certificate IDs
  • Institutional cohorts and department dashboards
  • Audited evidence trail and appeal process

The reviewer model is the most differentiated future idea — but it carries real operating burden (qualifications, training, conflict policy, rubric, evidence trail, appeals, verification, revocation). Keep it as the future standard; for v1, say "Foundation learners complete reviewed work" and "every paid artifact follows a review-ready structure," not "every credential names a reviewer."
