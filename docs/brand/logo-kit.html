01 / Architecture

How the two marks relate

Same construction, two lengths. Reach for the full wordmark first; drop to the abbreviation only when space or shape forces it.

Marketing, site nav, covers, certificates, documents, email signatures. The default. No container.

[Ai]BI

App icons, favicons, avatars, badges, partner walls, footer chips — anywhere the full name won't read. Brackets wrap Ai; BI trails outside the brackets.

07 / Misuse

What breaks the mark

One accent, one italic, one construction. These are the failures to watch for.

✕Aı
No dotless stem + gold tittle. The i is a real italic serif glyph in one ink color.
✕[Ai]BI
Don't gold the letters. Gold belongs to the brackets only — never the Ai or BI.
✕[Ai]BI
Don't stretch or condense. Scale uniformly; never distort the proportions.
✕[Ai]BI
Don't sit full-color on gold. Use the navy tile or a mono variant on gold fields.
✕[Ai]BI
Don't change the weight. The wordmark is Inter 600 — not light, not black.
✕[Ai]BI
Don't fake the italic. The i is Instrument Serif italic — not a slanted Inter i.
