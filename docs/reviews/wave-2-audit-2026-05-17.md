## Auth flows — full Playwright suite

| Before Wave 2 | After |
|---------------|-------|
| 5 / 15 passing | **15 / 15 passing** |

Two underlying changes unblocked the rest:

1. **Playwright wasn't loading `.env.local`.** Next.js loads it
   automatically for `next dev`, but Playwright runs as a separate
   Node process and only inherited the shell env. Added
   `loadEnvConfig(process.cwd())` from `@next/env` to `playwright.config.ts`.
   Once that landed, all 5 logged-in flow tests started seeding correctly.

2. **`/auth/login` and `/auth/signup` didn't redirect logged-in
   visitors away.** New helper at
   `src/lib/auth/requireGuestOrRedirect.ts` mirrors the inverse of
   the `/dashboard` layout pattern; per-route layouts at
   `src/app/auth/login/layout.tsx` and `src/app/auth/signup/layout.tsx`
   call it. Preserves `?next=` if it was in the URL when the visitor
   arrived.
