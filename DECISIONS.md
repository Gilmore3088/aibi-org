## Assessments — free vs paid

**One readiness framework, two products. "Same map, two resolutions."**

1. **Canonical framework = the 8 dimensions** (the paid In-Depth / v4 set). Both the
   free and the paid assessment score the **same 8 dimensions**:
   1. Approved AI Access
   2. Model Oversight
   3. Compliance Clarity
   4. Data Safety
   5. Workflow Fit
   6. Human Control
   7. Vendor Control
   8. People & Governance

2. **Free assessment = 12 questions.** Non-negotiable. The free assessment asks
   **12 questions**, mapped onto the 8 canonical dimensions (some dimensions get
   more than one question). Do **not** change it to 8 questions, or any other
   count. "12 questions in ~3 minutes" is the free experience.

3. **Free and paid stay distinctly separate products.** Same 8-dimension spine, but:
   - **Free** — 12 questions, ~3 min, no login, individual voice, a snapshot
     (score + top gap + a taste of the plan). Free. Top of funnel.
   - **Paid In-Depth** — 48 questions (6 per dimension), login-gated, deeper
     diagnostic (per-dimension root cause, 90-day action plan with owners,
     work-product prompts, reviewer packet). $99.
   Do not merge the two funnels; only the framework underneath is shared.

4. **Scoring + bands are shared** so a free result previews the paid one: both
   normalize to the same scale and use the same maturity-band ladder (a band name
   means the same thing in both). _(Implementation target — see the unification
   plan; free is being re-based onto this.)_

5. **No peer / percentile / cohort comparison. Anywhere.** The peer-band feature
   was removed from the report on 2026-06-01 because the source data was
   unverified. Do not advertise, imply, or render "peer-band comparison,"
   "percentile," "top decile," "X% of community banks," or "vs. cohort" in any
   assessment surface. (Sourced, attributed external stats — e.g. named Gartner
   Peer Community survey figures in resource articles — are fine; they carry a
   citation.)

## Brand

6. **The header wordmark is the full "[Ai] Banking Institute" lockup** at every
   width, including mobile. It is **never** truncated to a compact "[Ai]BI" form.
   If it doesn't fit, scale it down / tighten spacing — do not shorten the name.

## Terminology

7. **The deliverable is a "starter artifact."** Use "starter artifact"
   ("Your starter artifact" as the label). Do not reintroduce "first working
   template," "30-day starter move," or "30-day starter artifact."
