BSA / AML Operating Model

The investigator's actual loop.

Detect. Investigate. Document. Decide. Each step has an artifact and a name attached.

01

Detect

Alert hits from the monitoring system, frontline referral, or manual review. Each gets a case ID and an owner the same day.

Artifact produced
Case open + owner assigned
02

Investigate

Pull transaction history, KYC file, related accounts. AI summarizes patterns; analyst confirms, reads, and decides what's noise.

Artifact produced
Pattern summary + analyst notes
03

Document

Either draft the SAR narrative or write the no-SAR memo. The decision must be defensible at exam regardless of direction.

Artifact produced
SAR narrative or no-SAR memo
04

Decide

BSA officer signs off. SARs filed within 30 days of detection (60 if no suspect). Decision is logged with reviewer.

Artifact produced
Filed SAR or closed case + sign-off

Walk the file

Four questions, in order

Based on FinCEN SAR-filing thresholds (31 CFR 1020.320) and the FFIEC BSA/AML Examination Manual.

Question 1
Is there an identifiable transaction or pattern that has no apparent lawful purpose, given what we know about this customer?
Question 2
Does the aggregate involve $5,000 or more (or any amount if insider abuse is suspected)?
Question 3
Is there a pattern that looks like structuring — multiple deposits or withdrawals just below the $10,000 CTR threshold, especially across days or branches?
Question 4
Is the activity unusual relative to the customer's known profile, occupation, and historical activity (CDD baseline)?

Scenario Practice

Three reps every BSA analyst should run.

Sandbox scenarios use synthetic transaction extracts — no real customer data, ever.

Active Sandbox Scenario
SAR Narrative · Structuring Pattern
Input
Synthetic case: 8 cash deposits between $9,200 and $9,800 over 11 business days, two branches, single personal account. Customer is a small-business sole proprietor whose CDD profile cites monthly cash deposits in the $2k–$4k range.
Prompt
Draft a SAR narrative using the FinCEN 5 Ws (who, what, when, where, why suspicious). Stay factual. Do not characterize intent. End with "BSA officer to confirm filing decision."
Expected Output
Chronological narrative, factual tone, total aggregate amount stated, deviation from CDD baseline stated, and explicit human-confirmation reminder. No legal conclusions.

Pre-file Checklist
Before any SAR or no-SAR closes
Narrative covers 5 Ws (who / what / when / where / why suspicious)
No characterization of intent or legal conclusion
Aggregate amount and date range stated explicitly
CDD baseline cited (or noted as missing — and refresh triggered)
Customer name, address, account number, SSN/TIN, DOB verified
No tipping — customer not informed a SAR is being considered
BSA officer signature recorded; filing within 30 / 60 day window
