Campaign Brief · Spring Auto Refi
Input
Product team wants a 4-week spring auto-refi push. Members within 25 miles. Goal: 80 funded refis. Rate floor 6.49% APR for tier-A credit.
Prompt
Draft a campaign brief: audience, offer, channel mix, KPI, deadline, product owner. Flag what's missing from the rate description that Reg Z would require.

Six-field brief + a flag noting "as low as 6.49% APR" requires Reg Z triggering-term disclosures (terms, fees, repayment).

Compliance Handoff

Send the packet that gets approved in one pass — not the asset that gets bounced three times.

Pre-handoff Checklist
Before sending a draft to compliance
Brief attached — six fields complete
Product-specific disclosure block included (Reg DD / Reg Z)
Every superlative ("best," "lowest") has a source file
Rate quotes accompanied by Reg Z triggering-term details
No "guaranteed approval" / "no credit check" / "free" without conditions
FDIC / NCUA mention in any deposit ad
AI assist disclosed in the version log if material
Evidence Pack

What marketing hands compliance.

Not the asset alone — the asset plus the proof. Designed for one-pass approval.

Evidence readiness
4-part packet

Brief, draft with disclosures, claim substantiation, and version log — bundled for the compliance reviewer's queue.

1

Campaign brief

Audience, offer, channel, deadline, KPI, product owner. Six fields, one page.

2

Draft + disclosure block

Copy attached to the Reg DD / Reg Z disclosure block its product requires.

3

Claim substantiation

For each superlative or quantitative claim, the source file and the date the claim is valid through.

4

Version log

Which drafts were AI-assisted, the human owner of each version, and the channels each is approved for.
