Evidence Pack

Make the compliance value visible.

The playbook shows exactly what evidence a learner produces for internal review, management, or exam readiness.

Evidence readiness
4-part packet

Use-case summary, data boundary, review standard, and retention rule.

1

Use-case summary

Business purpose, user role, tool used, and workflow owner.

2

Data boundary

What data may be entered, what data is prohibited, and what must be redacted.

3

Review standard

Who reviews the output, what they check, and when escalation is required.

4

Retention rule

Where outputs are stored, how long they are kept, and how reviewed versions are labeled.
