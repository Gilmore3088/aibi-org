Branch Operating Model

The loop a branch already runs — sharpened.

Listen for the moment. Resolve what you can at the line. Coach the rep. Track the trend.

01

Listen

Capture the member's words first — not the system code. AI helps the teller paraphrase back without losing the original.

Artifact produced

Resolve

Track

Roll complaints up into a weekly trend the branch manager presents at huddle — and into the bank's complaint program.

Scenario Practice

Three reps every branch should run.

Sandbox scenarios let the line practice with synthetic member moments before live encounters.

Active Sandbox Scenario
Service Recovery · Overdraft Fee
Input
Member at the window is upset about a $35 overdraft fee they say they didn't expect. Account in good standing for 9 years.
Prompt
Help me capture this conversation. Paraphrase the member's complaint in their words. Suggest one resolution I can offer at the window without a manager. List anything that needs disclosure (Reg DD, account terms).
Expected Output
Verbatim intake + member-language paraphrase + one in-line resolution option + Reg DD reference if a fee was disclosed in the schedule.
Recommended Training Path

What branch staff should take next.

Four modules built around the frontline shift, not generic AI literacy.

Listening Reps

Capture the member's words first. Paraphrase second. AI does the second part, not the first.

Reg E Clock

Know the 10 / 45 / 90-day rule cold. AI is a backstop, not a substitute, for the timeline.

Coaching Notes

One strength, one growth area, one follow-up. AI drafts; manager owns and signs.

Elder Red Flags

FinCEN EFE indicators. Reluctant beneficiary, sudden POA, unfamiliar new "advisor."

End-of-shift Checklist
Before the teller line closes
Every complaint captured in the log, not just the system
Any Reg E item routed to ops with date stamp
EFE red flags escalated to BSA with member name
No member PII typed into any non-approved AI tool
Member commitments noted for next-day follow-up
Coaching notes drafted, signed, shared with rep
Evidence Pack

What the branch produces from the playbook.

Branch evidence is what the manager carries into the regional review and what the complaint program rolls into the board report.

Evidence readiness
4-part packet

Complaint log, Reg E timing trail, coaching notes, and weekly branch trend — all standardized across the network.

1

Complaint log entry

Member words, category, channel, signals, resolution, owner, and routing path.

2

Reg E timing trail

Date of notice, provisional credit decision, investigation close — proves the 10 / 45 / 90 was honored.

3

Coaching note

One strength, one growth area, one follow-up. Signed by the rep and the manager.

4

Weekly branch trend

Complaint volume by category, repeat rate, and any EFE escalations — for huddle and the complaint program rollup.
