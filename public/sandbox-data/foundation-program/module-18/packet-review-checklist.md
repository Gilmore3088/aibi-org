# Packet Review Checklist

Use this before saving your final Foundation Packet summary.

## Usefulness

- The workflow is a real task from your role.
- The reusable asset has a clear purpose.
- A peer can tell when to use it.
- A manager can see who owns the final output.

## Safety Boundary

- Allowed inputs are named.
- Blocked inputs are named.
- Customer NPI is excluded or replaced with placeholders.
- Credit decisions, SARs, examiner material, and confidential strategy are blocked unless a formal approved system and review path exist.

## Review Control

- A human reviewer is named by role.
- The review happens before impact.
- The reviewer can approve, change, block, or escalate.
- The stop rule is specific enough to use.

## Evidence

- Source or input is retained or summarized.
- Prompt or skill version is visible.
- Raw AI output summary is retained.
- Human edits are explained.
- Final output owner is named.

## Reuse

- The packet names the first next use.
- The packet includes a review cadence.
- The packet says when it should not be reused.
- The packet is short enough for a manager or peer to inspect.
