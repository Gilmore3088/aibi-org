# Module 1: Messy Internal Email Drafts

Redacted practice notes for turning loose internal messages into action-first staff emails. Keep placeholders. Do not add facts.

---

## Draft 1: Fee Reversal Follow-Up

Original note:

> [MEMBER] called about a fee on [ACCOUNT REMOVED]. Branch said ops would look at it but nobody owned the follow-up. Need to tell branch what to do. Might be today? Also check whether this is the same issue from last week. Do not promise reversal yet.

Known details:
- Owner: Deposit Operations
- Deadline: today before branch close
- Human review: verify whether the fee meets policy before any member-facing message

## Draft 2: Rate Sheet Reminder

Original note:

> Rates changed again Monday. Some staff are still using the old sheet. Need everyone to stop quoting old numbers and use the posted sheet. Maybe send to branch managers and call center leads. Keep it calm.

Known details:
- Owner: Retail Administration
- Deadline: before tomorrow morning huddle
- Human review: confirm the current rate sheet link before sending

## Draft 3: Branch Huddle Note

Original note:

> Online banking assistant can answer basic hours and navigation questions, but it cannot authenticate a member or solve account problems. Staff keep saying "the AI can handle it" and that is too broad. Need a short huddle message.

Known details:
- Owner: Branch managers
- Deadline: this week's huddle
- Human review: confirm approved assistant capabilities before sharing

## Draft 4: Vendor Demo Recap

Original note:

> Vendor showed a tool that reads loan files and gives a risk summary. Some people liked it. Before anybody pilots it we need IT/security and lending ops to say what data class it touches. Could be useful but not ready.

Known details:
- Owner: Lending Operations with IT/Security
- Deadline: before any pilot account is created
- Human review: confirm vendor data handling and approved-use status

## Draft 5: Complaint Routing Cleanup

Original note:

> Customer care inbox has fee complaints, fraud reports, and loan questions mixed together. We need a cleaner routing note. Do not include customer examples. Make it clear fraud goes urgent, loan questions go lending support, fee complaints go branch/service manager depending on source.

Known details:
- Owner: Customer Care lead
- Deadline: [VERIFY]
- Human review: confirm routing rules with compliance before sending
