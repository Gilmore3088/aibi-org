# Safe AI Use Pledge — Template

> The signed statement that closes the AiBI-Foundation credential.
> Personal, specific, dated, and named. A generic pledge defeats the
> purpose — yours should name your role, the actual tools you use, the
> actual workflows you've built, and the actual human review owners.

---

## Pledge

I, [Your Name], pledge to use AI tools in my role at [Your Institution]
under the following commitments:

**Workflows I will use AI for**

I will use [Tool name(s)] for the following AI-assisted workflows:

- [Workflow 1 — name and outcome]
- [Workflow 2 — name and outcome]
- [Workflow 3 — name and outcome]

For each, my judgment is the final layer between AI output and use, and
a named human reviewer [name role] checks before the artifact reaches
its audience.

**Data I will not paste into any AI tool, regardless of approval status**

- Member names, account numbers, or any field that identifies a specific
  member
- Specific transaction amounts attached to a specific member
- Examiner correspondence or findings
- Board-confidential strategic decisions or non-public financials
- Any item in the institution's data classification policy marked Red

If a workflow appears to require pasting any of the above, I will
redesign the workflow with placeholders OR I will not use AI for that
workflow.

**How I will represent AI-assisted work**

I will not present AI-generated text as if I drafted it from scratch.
For artifacts that will be reviewed by an officer, a committee, or an
examiner, the review record will include the original AI output, my
annotations, and the named reviewer's sign-off.

**How I will keep this pledge current**

I will review this pledge quarterly. If my workflows change, the tools
I use change, the data I handle changes, or my role changes, I will
rewrite the pledge to match — not the other way around.
