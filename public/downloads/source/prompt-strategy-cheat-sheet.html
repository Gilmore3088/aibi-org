Prompt Strategy Cheat Sheet

A concise reference for safer, more useful AI prompts in banking. Use it to strip sensitive data, give the model enough context, request a reviewable format, and keep a qualified human in the loop.

Section 1 - Five-line prompt pattern

Use RCFC + R before asking AI to help

Role. Context. Format. Constraints. Review. If one line is missing, the output is harder to trust.
LinePrompt moveBanking use
RoleTell the AI what perspective to use."You are a branch operations trainer..."
ContextGive the task, audience, and business setting."This is for frontline staff before a Monday huddle."
FormatName the output shape."Return a 5-bullet checklist under 120 words."
ConstraintsSet limits and red lines."Do not invent rates, policy requirements, or deadlines."
ReviewState who checks it before use."A manager will verify before sending."

Copy-ready starter prompt

You are a [BANKING ROLE] helping with [TASK]. Context: [AUDIENCE], [SITUATION], [GOAL]. Format: [OUTPUT TYPE], [LENGTH], [ORDER]. Constraints: Use placeholders only. Do not invent facts, rates, fees, dates, legal conclusions, or policy requirements. Review: Mark anything uncertain as [VERIFY]. A qualified human will review before use.

Strip specifics

Replace names, accounts, amounts, dates, addresses, and case facts with placeholders.

Ask for evidence

Tell the AI to separate facts, assumptions, and items requiring verification.

Control the format

Require bullets, tables, checklists, or drafts that a reviewer can scan quickly.

Section 2 - Safety lane

Pick the lane before the prompt

Green - usually safe

No sensitive data and no customer-impacting output. Examples: fictional training scenarios, blank checklists, public-source summaries, role-play prompts, and generic policy-reading aids.

Rule: if it could be written on a break-room whiteboard, it is usually Green.

Yellow - approved tool plus human review

The output could be seen by staff, customers, leadership, or examiners. Examples: campaign copy, policy training, internal procedures, meeting summaries, and customer-facing drafts using placeholders.

Rule: if the output will influence work, a qualified person reviews it before use.

Red - do not use public AI tools

Customer-specific decisions, SAR or AML investigations, adverse-action reasoning, legal conclusions, authentication decisions, confidential exams, vendor contracts, credentials, or production system details.

Rule: if it affects a customer, regulatory filing, control, or credential, treat it as Red until Compliance approves the workflow.

Verification checklist

  • No customer or employee personal data.
  • No account, loan, card, login, token, or credential details.
  • No invented rates, fees, dates, disclosures, or policy requirements.
  • Facts are tied to an approved source or marked [VERIFY].
  • Customer-facing language has a qualified reviewer.
  • Regulatory, legal, fair-lending, BSA/AML, or security claims are escalated.
  • The output is saved only in an approved system.
  • The final owner is named before anyone acts on it.
