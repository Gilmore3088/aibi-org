Section 1 - The missing middle layer

Before a prompt becomes a workflow, make it safe and reusable.

Core promise

In five minutes, a banker should know the prompt type, allowed source, output format, data boundary, reviewer, and escalation trigger.

The Safe AI Use Checklist teaches the core habits: strip sensitive data, ask clearly, fact-check, and escalate risky decisions. The Workflow SOP Builder documents repeatable AI-assisted workflows before reuse. This guide sits between them: it teaches staff how to write a safe, review-ready prompt before that prompt becomes a shared team asset.

This is not generic prompt engineering. It is banker prompting: use placeholders, choose the right output format, keep AI in a draft-support role, mark uncertainty with [VERIFY], and route higher-risk work to a qualified reviewer.

The Banker Prompt Formula

Role

Who should AI assist?

Name the banker role or review lens.

Task

What should it produce?

Ask for draft support, not a final decision.

Source

What material may it use?

Use public, internal, redacted, or synthetic source material.

Format

What should the answer look like?

Choose bullets, table, checklist, email, memo, SOP, FAQ, or markdown.

Constraints

What must it avoid?

No invented facts, sensitive data, decisions, or unsupported claims.

Review

Who checks it before use?

Name the reviewer, [VERIFY] rule, and escalation trigger.

Section 3 - Safe placeholders

Replace real specifics before the prompt.

Use uppercase bracketed placeholders consistently across the bank's prompt library. The point is not to hide the review obligation. The point is to remove real sensitive facts before asking for draft support.

Use placeholderRepresentsReview note
[CUSTOMER]Customer or member referenceUse fictional names only in training scenarios.
[ACCOUNT_TYPE]Checking, savings, CD, HELOC, loan, or cardDo not include account ownership details unless approved.
[ACCOUNT_NUMBER]Account number or identifierNever paste real account numbers into public tools.
[DATE]Relevant date or date rangeUse only if timing matters and identity does not.
[AMOUNT]Balance, payment, fee, or transaction amountRanges are safer than exact values for examples.
[TRANSACTION_TYPE]Deposit, ACH, debit card, wire, check, or ATM eventUse pattern-level language where possible.
[BRANCH]Branch, team, or locationRemove employee or customer specifics.
[PRODUCT]Product or serviceConfirm terms against approved product material.
[NEXT_STEP]Human action, handoff, or open itemAI can draft the step; the owner confirms it.
[POLICY_SECTION]Approved policy or procedure referenceVerify section numbers and wording against source.
[REVIEWER]Human reviewer role or ownerName the real reviewer before downstream use.
[VERIFY]Uncertain, missing, unsupported, or source-dependent itemResolve before final use.

Placeholder warning

Placeholders reduce exposure, but they do not automatically make sensitive facts safe. If the story still reveals a real customer, investigation, SAR, complaint, loan file, fraud case, or security issue, escalate instead of prompting.

Unsafe versus safer prompt patterns.

Copy-ready starter prompt

You are assisting [ROLE] with [TASK]. Use only this approved, redacted, or synthetic source material: [SOURCE_DESCRIPTION]. Do not use or request customer names, account numbers, balances, transaction details, SSNs, DOBs, complaint specifics, SAR/AML case facts, authentication details, examiner-sensitive material, passwords, API keys, access tokens, or production system details. Return the answer as: [OUTPUT_FORMAT]. Constraints: Do not invent facts. Do not make a decision. Do not add requirements. Preserve source meaning. Mark uncertain or unsupported items as [VERIFY]. Label output as draft pending human review. Human review: [REVIEWER] confirms source accuracy, data handling, unclear items, final-use approval, and evidence retention. Escalate if [ESCALATION_TRIGGER].
Section 5 - Review before use

Require the six checks before copying output downstream.

  • Sensitive data removed or replaced with approved placeholders.
  • Tool is approved for this data class.
  • Prompt asks for draft support, not a final decision.
  • Output format is clear.
  • Uncertain items must be marked [VERIFY].
  • Reviewer and escalation trigger are named.
Move to SOP when repeated

If the same prompt becomes a team practice, move it into the Reusable AI Working Brief and then the Workflow SOP Builder. A reusable prompt needs an approved tool, allowed inputs, prohibited inputs, reviewer, approval checkpoint, escalation path, and retention rule.
