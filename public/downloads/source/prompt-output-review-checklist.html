Prompt Output Review Checklist

Before copy or download

Require all six checks before output moves downstream.

A good AI answer is still a draft. The reviewer must confirm that the input was safe, the tool was allowed, uncertainty is marked, and final use is allowed for the risk lane.

  • Sensitive data removed: Customer names, account numbers, balances, transaction details, SSNs, DOBs, complaint specifics, SAR/AML case facts, credentials, and examiner-sensitive material were removed or replaced with approved placeholders.
  • Tool approved: The AI tool is approved for this data class.
  • Draft support only: The prompt asked for draft support, not a final decision.
  • Format clear: The prompt named the output format: bullets, table, checklist, email draft, script, FAQ, SOP, memo, markdown, or evidence packet.
  • [VERIFY] required: Uncertain, unsupported, missing, or source-dependent items must be marked [VERIFY].
  • Reviewer and escalation named: The human reviewer and escalation trigger are clear before downstream use.
Move to SOP when repeated

If the same prompt becomes a team practice, move it into the Workflow SOP Builder. A reusable prompt needs an approved tool, allowed inputs, prohibited inputs, reviewer, approval checkpoint, escalation path, and retention rule.

Source basis: NIST AI Risk Management Framework, OWASP GenAI Top 10, Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships, CFPB Regulation B, CFPB Regulation P, FFIEC BSA/AML Manual risk concepts, and AiBI workflow SOP curriculum.

Adapt before adoption: This checklist is a training aid, not legal advice or an examiner-approved policy. Adapt it to your institution's data classification, records-retention schedule, review authority, and escalation procedures.
