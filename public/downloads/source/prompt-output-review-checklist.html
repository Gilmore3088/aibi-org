Before downstream use

Review the input, the output, and the final-use risk.

A good AI answer is still a draft. The reviewer must confirm that the input was safe, the output is accurate, uncertainty is marked, and final use is allowed for the risk lane.

  • Sensitive data removed: Customer names, account numbers, balances, transaction details, SSNs, DOBs, complaint specifics, SAR/AML case facts, credentials, and examiner-sensitive material were removed or replaced with placeholders.
  • Tool approved: The AI tool is approved for the data class and intended output.
  • Draft support only: The prompt asked for draft, summary, rewrite, extraction, comparison, checklist, critique, or scenario support, not a final decision.
  • Format controlled: The output is in the requested format: bullets, table, checklist, email draft, script, FAQ, SOP, memo, markdown, or evidence packet.
  • Facts verified: Numbers, dates, citations, policy claims, product terms, procedural steps, and compliance references were checked against source documents.
  • [VERIFY] resolved: Uncertain or unsupported items are marked [VERIFY], then confirmed, corrected, escalated, or removed before final use.
  • Reviewer named: Customer-facing, examiner-facing, regulated, retained, or operational output has a named human reviewer.
  • Escalation triggers clear: Customer impact, credit, BSA/AML, fraud, authentication, complaints, examiner-facing material, legal/compliance interpretation, or prohibited data routes to the right owner.
  • Evidence retained: Final version, source reference, reviewer note, approval record, and exception notes are retained where required.
Move to SOP when repeated

If the same prompt becomes a team practice, move it into the Workflow SOP Builder. A reusable prompt needs an approved tool, allowed inputs, prohibited inputs, reviewer, approval checkpoint, escalation path, and retention rule.

Source basis: NIST AI Risk Management Framework, OWASP GenAI Top 10, Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships, CFPB Regulation B, CFPB Regulation P, FFIEC BSA/AML Manual risk concepts, and AiBI workflow SOP curriculum.

Adapt before adoption: This checklist is a training aid, not legal advice or an examiner-approved policy. Adapt before adopting it to your institution's data classification, records-retention schedule, review authority, and escalation procedures.
