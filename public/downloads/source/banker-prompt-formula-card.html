Prompt structure

Context. Objective. Resources. Expectations.

Use CORE before every work prompt. It helps the banker define the job, strip sensitive inputs, require a useful format, and keep responsibility with a human reviewer. Skip a part and you can predict what breaks.

Context

Who is AI helping, and for whom?

Example: "You are assisting a branch operations trainer preparing material for new tellers." Do not ask AI to act as the final approver, lender, investigator, compliance officer, or legal authority.

Objective

What exactly should it produce?

Draft, summarize, rewrite, extract, compare, checklist, critique, scenario, data-to-narrative, or solution brief. Name the task, not just the topic. Use draft support, not decisions.

Resources

What approved material may it use?

Name the public, internal, redacted, or synthetic source, and tell it not to guess beyond it. Use placeholders for anything sensitive. If the source contains sensitive data, stop and reclassify before prompting.

Expectations

What shape, what limits, who checks it?

Pick the format first: bullets, table, checklist, email draft, script, FAQ, SOP, memo, or evidence packet. Do not invent facts, rates, fees, dates, policy requirements, legal conclusions, fraud findings, or credit reasons. Mark uncertain items [VERIFY]. Name the reviewer and the escalation trigger before copying output downstream.

Copy-ready starter

Context: You are assisting [ROLE] with work for [AUDIENCE]. Objective: [TASK: draft, summarize, rewrite, extract, compare, or checklist]. Resources: Use only [APPROVED SOURCE]. Do not use or request prohibited data. Expectations: Return [OUTPUT FORMAT]. Do not invent facts or make a decision. Preserve source meaning. Mark uncertain items [VERIFY]. [REVIEWER] will review before final use. Escalate if [TRIGGER] appears.

Source basis: NIST AI Risk Management Framework, OWASP GenAI Top 10, Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships, CFPB Regulation B, CFPB Regulation P, FFIEC BSA/AML Manual risk concepts, and AiBI safe-use curriculum.

Adapt before adoption: This card is a training aid, not legal advice or an examiner-approved policy. Adapt it to your institution's approved AI tools, data classes, review authority, and retention rules.
