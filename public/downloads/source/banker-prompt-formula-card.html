The Banker Prompt Formula

A one-page structure for turning vague AI requests into safe, reviewable banker prompts.

The AI Banking Institute - 2026

Prompt structure

Role. Task. Source. Constraints. Output. Verify. Escalate.

Use this formula before every work prompt. It helps the banker define the job, strip sensitive inputs, require a useful format, and keep responsibility with a human reviewer.

Role

Who should AI act as?

Example: "You are assisting a branch operations trainer." Do not ask AI to act as the final approver, lender, investigator, compliance officer, or legal authority.

Task

What should it produce?

Draft, summarize, rewrite, extract, compare, checklist, critique, scenario, data-to-narrative, or builder brief. Use draft support, not decisions.

Source

What approved material should it use?

Name the public, internal, redacted, or synthetic source. If the source contains sensitive data, stop and reclassify before prompting.

Constraints

What must it not do?

Do not invent facts, rates, fees, dates, policy requirements, legal conclusions, fraud findings, or credit reasons.

Output

What format should the answer use?

Bullets, table, checklist, email draft, script, FAQ, SOP, memo, markdown, or evidence packet. Pick the format before generating.

Verify and escalate

What needs human review?

Mark uncertain items [VERIFY]. Escalate customer impact, credit, BSA/AML, fraud, authentication, complaints, examiner-facing material, legal/compliance interpretation, or prohibited data.

Copy-ready starter

You are assisting [ROLE] with [TASK]. Use only [APPROVED SOURCE]. Do not use or request prohibited data. Return [OUTPUT FORMAT]. Do not invent facts or make a decision. Preserve source meaning. Mark uncertain items [VERIFY]. [REVIEWER] will review before final use. Escalate if [TRIGGER] appears.

Source basis: NIST AI Risk Management Framework, OWASP GenAI Top 10, Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships, CFPB Regulation B, CFPB Regulation P, FFIEC BSA/AML Manual risk concepts, and AiBI safe-use curriculum.

Adapt before adoption: This card is a training aid, not legal advice or an examiner-approved policy. Adapt before adopting it to your institution's approved AI tools, data classes, review authority, and retention rules.
