What the score means

A score of 62 places this institution in the Structured Beginner band. The bank has informal AI use across departments, an executive conversation underway, and the beginnings of a governance posture — but no written framework, no inventory of use cases, and no documented review path. This is the most common tier for community banks and credit unions today, and the easiest to move out of.
