A score of 36 out of 48 places this sample banker in the Building Momentum band. The pattern is not beginner-level awareness. One concrete next step

Every free readiness snapshot ends with one starter artifact tied to the lowest-scoring readiness signal. The sample artifact below is what a banker with a Documentation gap could use this week.

Starter artifact · AI recordkeeping template

Create one place to save AI-assisted work. For each item, capture six fields: date, task, tool, prompt, unedited output, and the edits or judgment applied before use. This is not bureaucracy. It is the evidence trail that lets a manager, reviewer, or examiner reconstruct what AI did and what the human decided.

What you would do with this real report

  1. Read the score and tier once, then focus on the top gap.
  2. Use the starter artifact on one real task this week.
  3. Share the before/after with a manager or reviewer so the AI work becomes visible and repeatable.
