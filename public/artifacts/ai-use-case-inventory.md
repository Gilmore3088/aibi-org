# AI Use-Case Inventory

A one-page register of every AI-touched workflow inside your institution.
Maintain it. Bring it to your next AI committee meeting. Hand it to your
examiner before they ask.

## Why this exists

The AIEOG AI Lexicon (US Treasury / FBIIC / FSSCC, February 2026) names the
"AI use case inventory" as a baseline expectation for institutions of any
size. SR 11-7 (Federal Reserve / OCC) treats AI tools as models — and models
in production must be inventoried. The cheapest examiner-readiness move in
community banking is keeping this list current.

## How to use this template

1. Survey every department. Ask the same question twice: "Are you using AI
   for any work task?" and "Is any vendor providing you AI-powered features
   you may not have classified as AI?"
2. Capture one row per workflow, even when the same tool covers multiple
   workflows.
3. Review quarterly. Add new workflows; retire dormant ones; update the
   oversight column when a workflow's risk profile changes.

## Column definitions

- **Workflow** — the actual task, in plain language. Not the system name.
- **AI tool** — the specific product and version. "Microsoft 365 Copilot"
  not "Microsoft."
- **Data class** — Green (public), Yellow (internal non-NPI), Red (NPI /
  PII / loan files).
- **Owner** — a person, not a role.
- **Oversight** — automated (no review), sampled (random spot-check), or
  mandatory (every output reviewed).
- **Last reviewed** — the date a human verified that the row still
  describes how the workflow actually runs.
