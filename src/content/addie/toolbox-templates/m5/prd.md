## The data-discipline rule still applies — louder

When you paste this PRD into a prototyping tool, you are pasting it
into an AI tool. Everything Module 0 said about not pasting customer
data applies. Build the prototype against **synthetic data** —
invented names, account shapes, amounts. The PRD itself should not
contain a real member, a real account number, or a real internal
report.
