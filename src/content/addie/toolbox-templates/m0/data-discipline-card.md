## The one rule

Never put customer or confidential data into an AI tool.

No names tied to accounts. No account, card, or Social Security numbers.
No customer financials. No material non-public information.

> If you would not say it aloud in a crowded elevator, it does not go in.

---

## The move that keeps you working

**Describe the situation, not the person.**

- Do not type: "Customer Jane Doe, account 4471, balance twelve hundred
  dollars, is furious about an overdraft fee."
- Type: "A customer is upset about an overdraft fee and feels it was
  unfair. Help me draft a calm, empathetic reply that explains the fee
  and offers next steps."

Same help. Zero risk.
