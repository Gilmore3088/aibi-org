## The six modules

1. **Module 0 — Orientation.** Free. How this works + the one rule.
2. **Module 1 — Awareness.** Free. What this stuff actually is.
3. **Module 2 — Access & Workflow.** Free. Choose a tool and use it well.
4. **Module 3 — Prompting.** Free. The craft of asking. Ends at the gate.
5. **Module 4 — Skills.** Paid. Reusable skills and small workflows.
6. **Module 5 — Build.** Paid. Frame a problem and ship a working prototype.

---

## The gate (after Module 3)

After Module 3 you choose one of three doors:

- **Pay.** Buy the Foundation Course at $295 and finish all six modules.
- **Email to keep.** Give an email and keep everything in your Toolbox
  from the free side. Nothing saves anonymously.
- **Decline.** Walk away with what you learned. The $99 Readiness
  Assessment is available any time if you want a structured plan.

---

## No credential in v1

Completion is tracked but not marketed as a credential in this release.
The work product is the Toolbox.
