## Standing rules I am bringing to every tool on this map

- No member or customer data in a public tool. Ever. Anonymise the situation or do not send it.
- No credentials, secrets, or production system data in a public tool.
- No material non-public information in any tool that is not contracted to handle it.
- Every load-bearing fact or citation in an output gets verified against the actual source before it leaves my desk.
- A human reads anything that goes to a member.
