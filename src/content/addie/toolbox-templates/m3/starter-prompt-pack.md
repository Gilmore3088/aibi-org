## The one rule that still governs all of this

Never put customer or confidential data into an AI tool. No names tied
to accounts. No account, card, or Social Security numbers. No customer
financials. Nothing material that is not public yet.

> If you would be uncomfortable reading it aloud in a crowded elevator,
> it does not go in.

The move that keeps you working: **describe the situation, not the
person.** Generalize the case. Strip the identifiers. Get the help.

The Foundation Course sandbox blocks sensitive paste patterns. The real
tools you use outside the course will not. The discipline is yours.
