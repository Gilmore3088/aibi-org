# Module 4: Safe Work Profile Examples

Profile context should help AI understand the work without exposing customers, strategy, or confidential records.

## Profile A: Overexposed Draft

I manage the downtown branch. Last week [MEMBER NAME REMOVED] complained about account [ACCOUNT REMOVED], and our team is under pressure because the board is reviewing branch consolidation options. I want AI to write in my voice, mention that we are trying to protect retention, and use examples from recent complaints when drafting staff notes.

Problems to review:
- Customer complaint details are too specific.
- Internal strategic pressure is not reusable AI context.
- Recent complaints should not become examples in a general profile.

## Profile B: Safer Draft

I manage a retail branch team. My common AI-supported tasks are internal huddle notes, staff reminders, and first drafts of non-customer-specific training messages. Use clear, calm, plain-language wording. Do not include customer examples, account details, non-public strategy, or promises to members. Assume a human manager reviews every message before use.

Strengths to preserve:
- Role and audience are clear.
- Deliverables are reusable.
- Boundaries are explicit.
- Human review is built in.

## Profile C: Needs Tightening

I work in lending and need help with borrower communication, loan committee notes, and exception tracking. Keep the tone professional. You can use loan details if needed.

Problems to review:
- "Borrower communication" is too broad.
- "Loan details if needed" is unsafe without an approved system and placeholders.
- Review owner and do-not-use rules are missing.
