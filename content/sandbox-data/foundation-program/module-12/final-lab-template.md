```
[Paste the final, edited text here.]
```

**Annotations** — for each meaningful change you made:

- **Change:** [What you changed.]
  **Why:** [The specific reason.]

Example:
- **Change:** "Replaced 'we are pleased to report' with 'March net
  interest margin contracted to within our planning range'."

  reporting good news. The replacement leads with the data and lets
  the CFO judge tone."

> "I made it better" is not an annotation. Annotations name the specific
> change AND the specific reason. The annotations are where the
> credential is earned.
