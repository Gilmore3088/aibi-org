# Module 8: Workflow Map Scenario

Map this recurring workflow from trigger to archive. AI can assist, but it cannot own the decision.

## Current Workflow: Monthly Exception Narrative

Trigger:
- Month-end exception report is exported.

Current steps:
- Analyst reviews unresolved exceptions.
- Analyst drafts narrative for manager.
- Manager rewrites the narrative.
- Manager asks owners for missing updates.
- Final narrative is attached to the monthly operations packet.

Possible AI assists:
- Summarize sanitized counts by age bucket.
- Draft a first narrative from source fields.
- Turn manager comments into clearer owner requests.

Human checkpoints:
- Analyst verifies counts against the report.
- Manager approves final narrative.
- Operations director reviews exceptions older than 30 days.

Risk points:
- AI may invent root causes.
- AI may hide missing owner updates.
- AI may write as if unresolved items are closed.

Evidence to retain:
- Sanitized input fields
- Raw AI draft
- Human edits
- Final approval note

Blocked actions:
- Closing exceptions automatically
- Sending customer notices
- Assigning staff performance conclusions
